Shared prompt: Photorealistic 3:2 front-elevation Nordic interior architecture photograph. Straight-on empty central off-white plaster wall with soft natural daylight. Pale oak and authentic wood grain; no text, people, logos, watermarks, artwork or frames. Keep furniture low to leave usable wall space for separately composited artwork.

Living room: Solid oak wooden-frame sofa with visible wood arms/legs, cream linen cushions, small oak coffee table and subtle ceramics.
Study: Long low pale-oak writing desk, tucked wooden chair, minimal books and ceramic cup at the edge.
Entry: Low solid oak console, woven basket underneath, small ceramic vase to the side, pale timber floor.

The site identifies scenes as AI-generated. The calligraphy is composited from the supplied original, without regenerating the strokes. Scenes do not establish real-world scale.
